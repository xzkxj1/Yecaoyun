## Yecaoyun Introduction

Yecaoyun, established in 2012, operates under LucidaCloud Limited (Hong Kong Registration No. 2736053). The company is a member of both the Asia-Pacific Network Information Centre (APNIC) and the European IP Network Coordination Centre (RIPE NCC), demonstrating its commitment to professional internet infrastructure management.

With over 13 years of operational experience, Yecaoyun has carved out a niche serving small to medium-sized businesses and individual customers. The company specializes in providing hosting solutions for various scenarios including development testing, personal projects, cross-border e-commerce, gaming, and international office operations.

What sets Yecaoyun apart is its self-built 30Gbps multi-line BGP network in Hong Kong, featuring direct connections to mainland China and international carrier peering. This network architecture ensures excellent speed for users both within China and overseas. The company's product lineup includes cloud servers, VPS hosting, dedicated servers, colocation services, IP transit, web hosting, and domain registration.

The company's vision centers on reducing internet product operational costs for customers, enabling higher profits and lower startup costs for individual entrepreneurs and small-to-medium enterprises. Their slogan reflects this philosophy: "野草闲花次第生" (Wild grass and idle flowers grow in succession) - emphasizing growth and accessibility for all.
